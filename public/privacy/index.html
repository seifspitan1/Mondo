Privacy,
plain and simple.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

When you send a project brief

We store the name, email address, game information, and any optional details you submit. MONDO uses these details to understand your project, prepare a scope and quote, and contact you about your enquiry.

Who can see your information

Project briefs are available to the studio administrator. Your contact details and private brief are not published in the portfolio. The website’s hosting services process this information to operate the site.

Simple website activity

We count project opens, trailer plays, form starts, and successfully received briefs to understand how the website is used. These activity records do not include your name, email, or project description. No advertising trackers are added by MONDO.

Site access

During private review, and for studio administration, authentication uses secure, essential session cookies. The studio administrator’s credentials are used to protect administration access.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep enquiries to handle current discussions and follow up on agreed work. You can ask to correct or delete your enquiry using our project form. Include the email address you originally used and your request reference, if available.

Your choices

Optional form fields can be left empty. Sending a brief starts a project conversation and does not subscribe you to a marketing newsletter. You can ask us to stop contacting you.
