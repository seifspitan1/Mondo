04 BEFORE WE CREATE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Have something else in mind?

Let’s talk about it
What do you need to get started?

Send your game link or a description of the core gameplay, the assets you need, and any references. If your game is not live yet, a short overview is enough to start the conversation.

Can I order a thumbnail or trailer on its own?

Yes. Individual thumbnails and game trailers are available alongside launch packs and ongoing creative plans. Tell us what you need and we’ll scope the project with you.

How do the monthly plans work?

Each plan pairs a weekly thumbnail allowance with a monthly trailer allowance. The creative calendar, trailer length, revisions, and asset requirements are agreed before work begins.

What about delivery times and revisions?

Delivery dates and included revision rounds are confirmed in your project quote. Scope and timing depend on the creative direction, available assets, and the number of deliverables.

Do you capture gameplay or use my footage?

We agree on the capture approach in the brief. Let us know whether you already have footage, game access, character rigs, and other assets so the quote covers the work you need.

Can you guarantee performance?

Creative work is designed to attract the right attention. Results also depend on your audience, distribution, and game experience. When you share testing data, we can use it to inform future concepts.
